The best lap was deliberately conservative at T9 — more trail-brake, less throttle, lower apex speed. The trade-off paid off in the *next* sector: T10 South Bend recorded a series-best apex (87.9 mph) and the S5 sector dropped to a new record (12.689s). The 5.9 mph deficit at T9 is the cost of buying ~0.190s in South Bend. **This is a correct line trade, not a technique error** — but it does mean the optimum lap could combine T9-fast + South-Bend-fast on a different lap.
