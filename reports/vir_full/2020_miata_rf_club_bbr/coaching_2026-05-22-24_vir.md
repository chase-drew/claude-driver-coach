The four-weekend hesitation pattern at T17 is broken. **72% throttle at the apex on the series best lap** is the first time the series-leading lap has exceeded 70% throttle at Hog Pen. The style is early with 20% brake still applied at the apex and 72% throttle simultaneously — trail-braking through the apex into power, rather than the late-entry, hesitate-then-drive pattern that characterized every prior best lap.

The trade-off is visible: apex speed dropped 2.5 mph versus May 3 (69.3 vs 71.9 mph), because the early line sacrifices some entry speed. And the S9 sector is +0.102s slower despite the throttle gain. The culprit is T16: apex speed there dropped 1.5 mph, suggesting the early T17 entry is compromising the preceding corner's exit. The throttle commitment itself is correct and must be protected. The line needs refinement upstream.

## Improvement Targets — Next Weekend

### 1. S2 NASCAR — break 16.400s (two consecutive misses)

**Series best:** 16.319s (May 3). **This weekend:** 16.529s (second-worst in the series). T3 apex speed on the best lap was 65.2 mph versus May 3's 66.6 mph (−1.4 mph), which suggests entry braking is happening slightly early. NASCAR is a long-radius left that rewards carrying apex speed — a 1.4 mph apex deficit compounds across 180° of corner.

**On-track focus:** Establish a firm brake reference for T3 and compare to the last time the sector ran sub-16.400s (May 3 PM session). If no reference exists, delay the T3 brake point 5–8m on two consecutive laps and observe whether apex speed recovers toward 66+ mph. Target: ≤16.400s.

### 2. S9 Hog Pen — lock in the T17 breakthrough and fix T16 (target ≤11.900s)

**Series best:** 11.859s (May 3). **This weekend:** 11.961s (+0.102s regression despite T17 improvement). T17 throttle is solved — 72% is the new floor, not a ceiling. The sector regression points to T16: apex speed there was −1.5 mph, indicating the early T17 entry line is compromising the T16 exit. The two corners share a transition and the line for one affects the other.

**On-track focus:** With T17 early entry locked in, work T16 independently. A slightly later T16 apex may allow better exit speed heading into the T17 braking zone without disturbing the T17 commitment. Target: ≥70% throttle at T17 maintained, S9 ≤11.900s.

### 3. Merge the S4 and S5 ceilings onto the same lap (theoretical target: ≤2:16.200s)

**Context:** Day 3's theoretical optimum (2:16.287) contains the weekend's fastest S4 (18.960s, from L6 on the CAN-dropout lap) and Day 2 contains the weekend's fastest S5 (12.689s, L28★). These have never appeared on the same lap. The current series best lap trades T9 apex speed to win S5; the fastest Esses time comes from a different lap with a different line. If both peaks are independently achievable on the same tire set and conditions, they should be combinable onto one lap.

The arithmetic is favorable: if the series best lap (2:17.869) gained the S4 improvement (−0.191s, from 19.151s to 18.960s) without losing S5, the result would be ~2:17.678 — a meaningful step deeper into record territory. That doesn't require unlocking any new sector; it only requires carrying the T9 apex speed without disturbing the South Bend line.

**On-track focus:** Carry 106+ mph through T9 and observe what T10 apex speed results. If T10 stays above 85 mph with the higher T9 entry, the line conflict is minimal and the optimum is within reach on a future lap. If T10 falls below 83 mph at T9 speed, the conservative-T9 approach on L28★ is correct — and further gains in S4 require unlocking T7/T8 rather than T9. Either outcome is diagnostic.

## What to Keep Doing

**T14a Roller Coaster brake point:** The latest brake point in the session (106.8m vs 142m median) is producing the largest single-corner gain in the series. 80.0 mph at T14a is now the floor — do not let it drift back toward the 70–72 mph range from earlier weekends.

**T10 South Bend early entry + immediate throttle:** 87.9 mph with 100% throttle at the apex is the best South Bend this series has seen. Five straight weekends of improvement confirm the early style is correct. Protect this.

**T17 throttle commitment:** 72% at the T17 apex is the new reference point. Even while the line upstream is being refined, do not let throttle@apex drift back toward the 38–45% range that defined every prior series-best lap.

**Session building:** 4.6s std dev on Day 2 over 20 laps is the best multi-session consistency in this series. The series best lap arrived at L28 — built across an entire session of progressive refinement. Continue building within sessions rather than hunting for a single hot lap early.
