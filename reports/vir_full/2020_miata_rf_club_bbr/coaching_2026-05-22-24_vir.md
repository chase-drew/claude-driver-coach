## What to Keep Doing

**T14a Roller Coaster brake point.** The latest brake point in the session (106.8m before apex vs 142m median) is producing the single biggest corner gain in the series — a 5.7 mph apex-speed jump after four flat weekends. 80.0 mph at T14a is now the floor. Protect it.

**T10 South Bend early entry + immediate full throttle.** 87.9 mph at the apex with 100% throttle is the fastest South Bend this car has produced. **T10 apex speed has improved every weekend in the series** (80.9 → 87.9 mph over five weekends). The early style is correct.

**T17 throttle commitment.** 72% at the T17 apex on a series-best lap is a real technique shift. Even while the line upstream needs work, do not let throttle@apex drift back toward the 32–64% range that defined the four prior best laps.

**Day 2 session-building.** 4.6s std dev across 20 on-pace laps is the best multi-session consistency in the series, and the series best lap arrived at L28 — the 4th-to-last lap of the third segment. The series best wasn't a hot lap early; it was built. Continue building within sessions rather than chasing a single hot lap early.
