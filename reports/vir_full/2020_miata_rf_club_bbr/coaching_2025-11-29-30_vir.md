## Improvement Targets — Next Weekend

### 1. Consolidate the L26 Snake/South Bend on a faster overall lap (target: clip ≥ 0.4s off best)

**Current:** L27 (best, 2:18.888) ran S3 + S5 at 9.246s + 13.486s = 22.732s (derived).
**Achievable this weekend:** L26 (2:22.431 overall) ran S3 + S5 = 9.120s + 13.320s = **22.440s** — 0.292s faster than the best lap through those two sectors back-to-back.

L26 was 3.5s slower overall but clearly nailed the Snake → South Bend sequence. The on-track move is to bring L26's line through the Snake (T5b → T6a → T6b → T7 entry) and the South Bend approach (T10) onto a lap where the rest of the circuit holds the L27 pace. The Snake into South Bend is one continuous high-grip flow — owning it as one piece is more achievable than owning the individual sectors separately.

### 2. Hold L14's start→T1 carry-in speed without losing the rest of the lap (target: clip ≥ 0.15s off best)

**Current:** L27 ran the start→T1 + back-straight + front-straight (S1 + S7 + S10) at a combined ~57.0s (derived).
**Achievable this weekend:** L14 ran the same three sectors at **56.851s** — 0.15s+ faster.

L14's pedal channels are corrupted (99.8% reported throttle for the whole lap is impossible), so we can't read technique off that lap. But the **GPS-derived sector times are valid** — the car genuinely covered S1, S7, and S10 faster on L14. That means either (a) cleaner traffic and a slipstream on the back straight, or (b) different braking reference into T1 worth experimenting with. Worth a deliberate test: brake reference 5m later into T1 on a clean lap and see if the sector improves.

### 3. Compress the per-session warm-up phase

**Current state — Sunday session-by-session fast lap:** Sun-1 = 2:21.989 (L9, 9 laps in), Sun-2 = 2:20.040 (L13, 2 laps in), Sun-3 = 2:20.906 (L20, 2 laps in), Sun-4 = 2:18.888 (L27, 6 laps in).

The pace IS available early in a session (Sun-2 dropped under 2:20 by lap 2 of the session, Sun-3 by lap 2) — so the warm-up phase isn't a brake/tire-temperature limitation. **It's mostly Sun-1 (the very first session of the day) that takes 9 laps to find pace.** Sun-1 fast lap was at L9 of 11 — that's a long warm-up curve for a session starting on already-warm tires from the day's first out-lap onward.

**Action:** Target getting under 2:21 by lap 5 of Sun-1 next time at this track. The data signal will be Sun-1 fast lap arriving by L4–L5 of the session (instead of L9). That alone moves the absolute pace cluster earlier into the day, giving you more attempts at the 2:18s window.

**NASCAR (S2) is a strength.** L27 owned this sector at 16.507s — and it owns the series baseline. The line through T3 is working; don't experiment with this one.

**Hog Pen (S9) — L27 owns it at 12.000s** on the best lap. The T17 approach is working; this is also the slowest corner technique-wise so it's a good baseline reference for future T17 hesitation patterns.
