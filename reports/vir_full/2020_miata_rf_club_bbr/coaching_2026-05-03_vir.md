## Data Quality Notes

- **Small sample (9 on-pace laps total).** Sector optima are real but a single off-pace lap could have changed any of them. Cross-weekend comparisons here are directional.
- **PM session Steer RMS is 2–3× higher than AM** consistently across all 3 PM on-pace laps (314 / 333 / 395 vs AM range 108–149). This is either a partial steering-sensor drift in the PM session or genuine higher steering busyness — the data can't distinguish. **Don't read PM steering-smoothness as a technique signal** without follow-up.
